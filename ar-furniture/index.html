How It Works

From browsing to arranging, in four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Choose Furniture

    Select a chair, table, or bed from the catalogue.

  2. 2

    Preview with Marker

    Scan a furniture marker to view the 3D product in augmented reality.

  3. 3

    View in Your Room

    Use markerless AR to place the furniture inside your real room.

  4. 4

    Arrange Your Space

    Later, users will be able to move, rotate, customize, and arrange furniture.
